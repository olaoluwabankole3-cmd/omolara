01.
A few things about how I see the work

Point of view

Marketing is the meeting point between what you want to say and what people need to hear.

My background crosses engagement leadership, marketing representation, brand management and research. Each role sits in a different context, but they share a common thread: communicating ideas, connecting people and helping work move forward.

I’m drawn to work that starts with curiosity. Who is this for? Why should it matter to them? What would make the message easier to understand, remember or act on? Those are useful questions whether the setting is a brand, a community or a research initiative.

Working principleBe clear about the audience. Be deliberate about the message. Be honest about what the work achieved.
